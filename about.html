About the journal

Draping is written for people who love cloth that moves.

Draping began as a notebook of folds: ways to tie a scarf, wrap a shawl and pleat a skirt that we wanted to remember. It became an independent journal about the oldest technique in dress.

We write about how fabric hangs, how to style drapes on real bodies, and how to choose and care for cloth with movement. We cover everyday pieces such as scarves and wrap skirts alongside traditional drapes such as the saree, treating each with care.

We are independent. We do not sell clothes, accept payment for coverage or publish sponsored lists. Each article is checked by a second editor and dated when it is updated.
